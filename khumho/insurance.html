Insurance Claim Repairs

A servicing contractor that understands insurer processes, client requirements and the standards expected on every claim.

Our process

From assessment to sign-off

Every claim is handled with the accuracy and documentation insurers and incident managers expect.

  1. AssessOn-site inspection to evaluate the damage against the insured peril.
  2. Scope & quoteAn accurate scope of work and itemised quote with a photo report.
  3. RepairWork by qualified trades, compliant with NHBRC, PIRB and building regulations.
  4. Sign-offCompletion photos and compliance certificates where required, closing the claim cleanly.
