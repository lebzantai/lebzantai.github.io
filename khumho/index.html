Insurance & claims work

We know the claims process from the inside.

Both directors come from non-motor claims, with backgrounds in claims management, loss adjusting and quality assurance. We scope damage against insured perils fairly, document everything, and finish work the way insurers expect.

  1. AssessOn-site inspection and an accurate scope of the damage and its cause.
  2. Quote & reportA clear, itemised quote and photo report ready for your insurer or incident manager.
  3. RepairWork by qualified trades, compliant with NHBRC, PIRB and building regulations.
  4. Sign-offCompletion photos and a compliance certificate where required, closing the claim cleanly.
