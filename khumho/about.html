About Khumho Projects

A proudly South African construction company delivering quality building and maintenance services since 2012.

Who we are

Trusted builders, rooted in craftsmanship

Khumho Projects serves clients across Gauteng (Johannesburg and Pretoria) and the North West Province. We bring a practical, owner's-eye understanding of what makes a home or property built to last, and compliant with building and insurance requirements.

Leadership overview

Leadership background

100% Black owned and women led

The leadership team comprises two seasoned insurance-industry professionals with extensive experience in the non-motor claims environment. They specialise in assessing and loss adjusting by evaluating claims against insured perils, ensuring that damage is reviewed accurately and fairly and that the scope of work is correctly determined.

They manage projects in line with Treating Customers Fairly (TCF), supporting strong client outcomes, clear communication, and service quality aligned to insurer requirements. The team also ensures compliance with POPIA by maintaining client information responsibly throughout the claims process.
