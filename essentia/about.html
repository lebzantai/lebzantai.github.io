About Essentia360

A South African construction and maintenance company, formerly known as Mmakgarebe Projects. 100% female owned and managed.

Who we are

Quoted honestly, run safely, finished on time.

Essentia360, previously known as Mmakgarebe Projects, is a South African construction and maintenance company delivering roofing, building, plumbing and carpentry services to homeowners, businesses and property managers.

We are 100% female owned and managed, and we hold a BEE Level 1 rating. Our teams work to registered industry standards, are fully insured, and are backed by the qualifications and affiliations our clients rely on for compliant, sign-off ready work.
