How we work

From first visit to final sign-off

Every project follows the same clear process, with a written quote and agreed scope before work begins.

  1. Site assessmentWe inspect the work on site and confirm what needs to be done.
  2. Written quoteA detailed quote and agreed scope, with no surprise costs at handover.
  3. Work by registered tradesCarried out safely, to PIRB, NHBRC and PRAWA standards, and fully insured.
  4. Handover & sign-offA final walk-through, with certificates of compliance on qualifying work.
